About Us

A Clean Home. A Little More Peace of Mind.

At Sumbi’s Cleaning, we believe coming home to a clean, welcoming space is one of life’s simple joys. Our team works with care and attention to detail to help you enjoy that feeling.

Our company grew from hands-on experience working with other cleaning teams and learning what matters most to clients. For the past three years, we’ve put those lessons into practice through thorough cleaning, clear communication, and respect for every home we enter.

Thoughtful Care for Your Home

We treat your home and belongings with the care we would want for our own. We choose cleaning products carefully and use them according to the surfaces and product instructions.

We also listen to your priorities and explain what’s included in your service, so you know what to expect from our visit.

More Joy in Coming Home

Our goal is simple: to leave your home clean, fresh, and well cared for—and leave you happy with the work our team has done.

Because a clean home means more than a finished checklist. It means being able to relax and enjoy your space.
