Standard & Recurring Cleaning

Our recurring service helps keep your home clean, fresh, and well maintained. It covers kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, and living areas, following our Standard Cleaning checklist. Choose weekly, every two weeks, every three weeks, or monthly visits. Other intervals can be discussed.

Standard Cleaning checklist

Kitchen

  • Sanitize high-touch surfaces
  • Wipe & sanitize countertops
  • Polish granite countertops*
  • Clean stovetop
  • Clean inside & outside microwave
  • Clean exterior of appliances
  • Clean & polish sinks, faucets & fixtures
  • Spot clean cabinet fronts
  • Clean & polish exterior of range hood
  • Clean interior & exterior glass doors
  • Dust reachable light fixtures
  • Vacuum & mop hard floors
  • Empty trash & replace kitchen trash bag (provided by customer)

Bathrooms

  • Sanitize high-touch surfaces
  • Clean & sanitize toilets
  • Clean showers & bathtubs
  • Wipe & sanitize countertops
  • Clean & polish sinks, faucets & fixtures
  • Clean mirrors
  • Spot clean cabinet fronts
  • Dust blinds, sills & ledges
  • Clean interior windows
  • Dust reachable light fixtures
  • Vacuum & mop hard floors
  • Empty trash & replace trash liner

Bedrooms & Living Areas

  • Sanitize high-touch surfaces
  • Dust all accessible surfaces
  • Clean mirrors
  • Dust reachable light fixtures
  • Dust blinds, sills & ledges
  • Clean interior windows
  • Vacuum carpets & rugs
  • Vacuum & mop hard floors
  • Vacuum sofas & upholstered furniture
  • Make beds (upon request)
  • Empty trash & replace trash liner
  • Dust ceiling fans

*Granite surfaces only.

02

Deep Cleaning

A more intensive clean with extra time and attention throughout your home. Deep Cleaning includes everything in Standard Cleaning, plus detailed cleaning of baseboards and doors and accessible A/C and return vents. Recommended for first-time clients, seasonal refreshes, or homes that need extra care.

Deep Cleaning checklist

Everything in Standard Cleaning, plus:

  • Baseboards & Doors: Clean all baseboards and doors with a towel.
  • Air Vent Cleaning: Vacuum and clean all accessible air conditioning and return vents.
  • Deeper Attention: Extra time and attention to detail in every room for a truly deep clean.

Deep Cleaning is recommended for first-time clients, seasonal refreshes, or when extra care is needed.

Airbnb & Short-Term Rental Cleaning

Turnover cleaning and preparation for your next guests. Includes cleaning, laundry, replacing linens, and restocking owner-provided supplies according to your property’s agreed setup and instructions.

Our Airbnb Experience

We understand the responsibility that comes with preparing a short-term rental for incoming guests. With a full year of hands-on experience cleaning Airbnb properties, we pay close attention to detail and follow each owner’s specific requirements to help ensure the property is clean, properly stocked, and ready for the next arrival.

Every property is different. We’ll review your property setup and turnover priorities, then confirm the tasks included in your service.

Airbnb / Short-Term Rental checklist

Kitchen

  • Clean & sanitize countertops and high-touch surfaces
  • Clean stovetop
  • Clean microwave - inside & outside
  • Clean exterior of appliances
  • Clean & polish sinks, faucets & fixtures
  • Clean & polish exterior of range hood
  • Spot clean cabinet fronts
  • Restock owner-provided kitchen essentials as requested
  • Restock coffee, creamer, trash bags and other provided supplies according to the property’s requirements

Bedrooms & Living Areas

  • Dust & wipe accessible surfaces
  • Clean mirrors
  • Remove used bed linens
  • Make beds with clean sheets and duvets
  • Replace towels and linens according to the property setup
  • Dust ceiling fans
  • Vacuum carpets & rugs
  • Vacuum & mop hard floors

Bathrooms

  • Clean & sanitize toilets
  • Clean showers & bathtubs
  • Clean shower glass doors
  • Clean & sanitize countertops
  • Clean & polish sinks, faucets & fixtures
  • Clean mirrors
  • Replace clean towels
  • Restock owner-provided shampoo, conditioner, soap and toilet paper

Laundry & Turnover

  • Wash towels, sheets and duvets used by previous guests
  • Replace linens and towels throughout the property
  • Prepare beds and guest areas for the next arrival
  • Follow the owner’s specific laundry and turnover instructions

Property Preparation

  • Restock owner-provided guest supplies
  • Arrange items according to each property’s specific setup
  • Follow the owner’s individual checklist and special requirements
  • Pay close attention to the details needed before the next guest arrives

Commercial Cleaning

Keep your workplace clean, welcoming, and ready for the day. We provide cleaning for offices and other commercial spaces, with careful attention to shared areas, restrooms, break rooms, accessible surfaces, and floors.

Services may include dusting surfaces and ceiling fans, cleaning restrooms, wiping break room counters and appliance exteriors, emptying trash, vacuuming, and mopping. More detailed cleaning is available for spaces that need extra attention.

We’ll discuss your space, priorities, and preferred schedule, then confirm what’s included in your estimate.

Commercial Cleaning tasks

Offices & Shared Areas

  • Dust accessible surfaces
  • Clean mirrors
  • Dust reachable light fixtures and ceiling fans
  • Vacuum carpets and rugs
  • Vacuum and mop hard floors
  • Empty trash and replace liners

Restrooms

  • Clean toilets
  • Clean sinks, faucets and fixtures
  • Wipe countertops
  • Clean mirrors
  • Vacuum and mop hard floors
  • Empty trash and replace liners

Break Rooms

  • Wipe countertops
  • Clean sinks and faucets
  • Clean appliance exteriors
  • Clean microwave inside and outside, when included in the estimate
  • Spot clean cabinet fronts
  • Vacuum and mop hard floors
  • Empty trash and replace liners

Additional Detail Cleaning

  • Clean baseboards and doors with a towel, when included in the estimate
  • Vacuum and clean accessible A/C and return vents, when included in the estimate
  • Additional time and attention in agreed areas

The tasks above are available within the agreed scope of your commercial cleaning service. We’ll confirm the included areas, level of detail, and schedule in your estimate.
